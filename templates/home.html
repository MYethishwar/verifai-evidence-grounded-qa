How it works

From question to verified answer in five steps

Every answer goes through the same transparent pipeline, and every step is visible in its answer report.

Understand

Recognises greetings, new questions and follow-ups like "how is it treated?".

Check scope

Confirms the question is clinical; anything else is declined with an explanation.

Retrieve

Finds passages on the exact topic from the knowledge base and uploaded datasets.

Answer

Writes a broad, plain-language answer only from those passages, with citations.

Verify

Checks each sentence against the sources and reports how much is supported.
